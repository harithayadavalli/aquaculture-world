About Aquaculture

Aquaculture is the farming of aquatic organisms such as fish, shrimp and other aquatic plants in controlled water environments.

About Our Project

Aquaculture World is an educational project designed to provide information about fish farming, shrimp farming and sustainable aquaculture practices.

Project Objectives

  • To understand modern aquaculture practices.
  • To learn about fish and shrimp farming.
  • To understand water quality management.
  • To study real-world aquaculture activities through surveys.

Our Project Work

As part of this project, we explore aquaculture activities and collect information through student surveys and field observations.

Our Team

We are a team of students working together to explore and understand aquaculture. Through this project, we aim to learn about fish farming, shrimp farming and sustainable aquaculture practices.

Our Conclusion

Aquaculture plays an important role in food production and provides livelihood opportunities. By understanding modern techniques and proper water management, we can support sustainable aquaculture development.

Fish Farming

Fish farming is the process of raising fish in ponds, tanks and other controlled environments.

Shrimp Farming

Shrimp farming is an important aquaculture activity that involves growing shrimp in specially managed ponds.

Water Quality Management

Proper water quality, oxygen levels, pH and temperature are essential for healthy aquatic organisms.
